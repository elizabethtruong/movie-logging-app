About Movie Log

Movie Log is a personal movie journal that lives in your browser. No account or sign-up is necessary.

Log what you watch, rate it, write a review, and build a watchlist. Everything is saved locally on your device.

Log What You Watch

Search for any film using the OMDb database and log it in seconds. Each entry captures the title, poster, year, date watched, a rating out of five, and your own review.

  • Search OMDb to pre-fill title, year, and poster automatically
  • For films not in the database, enter a title manually
  • Edit or delete any entry from your Profile

Build a Watchlist

Save films you plan to watch. Search OMDb on the Watchlist page and add films directly to your list.

  • Add any OMDb film to your watchlist instantly
  • Films are automatically removed when you log them as watched
  • Your watchlist appears on both the Watchlist page and Dashboard

Explore Film Details

Click any film title throughout the site to open its full detail page. You'll see the complete plot, cast, director, runtime, release date, genre tags, and overall ratings from IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, and Metacritic.

Track Your Progress

The Dashboard shows four stats at a glance: films watched this month, this year, all time, and your average rating. As your log grows, your stats update automatically.

How It Works

Movie Log runs entirely in your browser. Your watch history and watchlist are stored in the browser's local storage. They stay on your device and persist between visits. No account is needed and nothing is uploaded to a server.

Film search and detail pages are powered by the OMDb API, which provides metadata, posters, and ratings for thousands of films.
